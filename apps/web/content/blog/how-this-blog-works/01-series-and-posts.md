A **series** is a focused topic told over several posts. On disk it is a folder
under `content/blog/`, and the posts inside are ordered by their numeric prefix.

## What you write

Each post starts with a few lines of frontmatter, then plain Markdown:

```md
---
title: Your post title
summary: One sentence that appears on the map and in lists.
date: 2026-11-16
---
```

Code blocks, lists, links and images all work.
