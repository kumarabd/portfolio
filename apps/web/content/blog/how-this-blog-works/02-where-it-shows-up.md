---
title: Where it shows up
summary: Every series becomes a branch on the map and a page of its own.
date: 2026-11-17
---

Run the dev server and the new series appears in two places:

- on the map, under **Writing**, as a bubble that opens into its posts;
- at `/blog`, as a plain reading list with a page per series and per post.

Set `draft: true` in a post's frontmatter to keep it out of both until it is ready.
